About Me

I am currently a final-year Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University, where I began a combined master's and Ph.D. program in the fall of 2021. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Duanqing Xu and Prof. Yabo Dong. Since December 2024, I have also been a visiting student at the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, under the supervision of Prof. Han Yu. I am now a research intern at Ant Group in Hangzhou, where I work on multimodal user behavior understanding and generation.

My primary research interests lie in sensor-based time-series data mining, including EEG, ECG, and human activity recognition, with a particular emphasis on developing LLMs for time-series analysis, generative modeling, and transfer learning. Now, I focus on sequential user behavior modeling, leveraging LLMs to understand user behavior and learn general-purpose representations for downstream decision-making in industrial applications.
